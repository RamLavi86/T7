Source: 2024 Yamaha Ténéré 700 Service Manual (LIT-11616-MC-B0), Ch. 3 — Periodic Maintenance. Tabs mirror the manual's own odometer columns: 1,000 km is the initial service; from 31,000 km onward the identical checklist pattern repeats every 6,000 km (per the manual's own note), so the 13,000 km tab's list is also what's due at 37,000 km, 43,000 km, and so on — the km-finder above works this out for any number you type.
